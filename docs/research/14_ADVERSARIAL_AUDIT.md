## Five-perspective review

A separate review pass is requested from chess research, quantitative markets, behavioral measurement, game design, and architecture/QA perspectives. Reviewer findings and dispositions are saved under `reviews/` when available. They are independent reasoning passes, not external expert certification. No review can confirm enjoyment or device feasibility without user/device testing.

## Retained risk policy

Do not conceal failures to preserve a polished narrative. Keep research-negative fixtures and exploit demonstrations in regression tests. A release gate can defer a subsystem while completing foundations, but cannot pretend an unverified core requirement is implemented. Scope is staged, not silently deleted.

## Final review disposition

All five requested independent perspective passes were performed by separate review agents and are retained verbatim in `reviews/`. They are reasoning reviews, not externally credentialed expert certification. Accepted corrections: explicit Crown adjudication/finite tiebreaks; mixed mate/CP mapping; fixed grade boundaries with separate uncertainty; monotone phase rules; descriptive skill-domain language and planned multiple-testing policy; conditional market liabilities and fixed cancellation payouts; paired calibration comparisons; funded integer-ledger tests; complete world topology/advance contract; costed club scenario; worker termination fallback; tick publish markers; short IndexedDB commit/fencing; quota-safe migrations. Source extraction's optional-tool behavior and experiment RNG independence were corrected. Game-length counts were fixed and experiments rerun.

Remaining findings are not waived: exhaustive/competition-policy rules validation, calibrated analytics, real item-bank validation, evolving world, integrated economy, actual mobile/Windows builds and human enjoyment studies remain gates. Historical review files may describe pre-correction text; this disposition and revised main documents are authoritative.

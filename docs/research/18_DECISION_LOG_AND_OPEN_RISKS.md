# Decision log and open risks

## Decisions

D01 Keep Crown & Calculation as the working name; no trademark clearance claimed.

D02 Academy-world core plus optional competition/exchange/institution branches, chosen by explicit subjective weighting/sensitivity, pending player tests.

D03 Los Alamos-derived competitive rules chosen for clear source/engine support; no balance superiority claim. Experimental bishop variant is not a historical variant.

D04 Prefer evaluated Fairy-Stockfish local WASM with GPL-compatible distribution, independent TypeScript rules oracle and custom fallback. Exact build/license/device feasibility remains a gate.

D05 Glicko-2 internal pools with period snapshots, persistent ledger and anchor calibration; no FIDE equivalence.

D06 Quality-loss accuracy and eight grades versioned, with experimental parameters disclosed until calibrated. Exceptional tactics require evidence, not material loss.

D07 Local deterministic motif/templates for coaching; no required local LLM or remote API.

D08 Full played/watched game histories, honest abstract-result background simulation; no fabricated replay.

D09 Uniform categorical fixed-b LMSR first, proper-score forecast companion, bounded conditional state spaces. No exogenous open-market recenter.

D10 Funded institutional/prize economy with explicit issuance/sinks and noncash recovery; baseline toy grants rejected as production tuning.

D11 Offline honest-play integrity only; no guarantee against the device owner. External exports required for genuine resilience to browser eviction.

D12 M1 durable records/rating/report foundation; M2 full intelligence; M3 civilization; M4 economics; M5 polished release. This preserves the earlier mandatory expansion's dependency priority.

## Ten most serious remaining risks

1. **Exact rule validation:** timeout, bare-kings adjudication and finite tiebreak rules are now explicit Crown policies, but user comprehension and production implementation verification remains untested. Blocks rated release until tested.
2. **Engine/device feasibility and license compliance:** source supports variant but pinned WASM build, strength, iOS thermals and Windows packaging unmeasured. Blocks performance/strength claims.
3. **Variant balance/depth:** random policies and tiny search matches cannot establish competitive viability or human enjoyment. Needs additional sourced variants, stronger mirrored matches and human tests.
4. **Analytics calibration:** expected-outcome mapping, grades, phase rules and Brilliant corpus are unvalidated. Blocks validated-accuracy claims; preserve experimental labeling.
5. **Psychometric validity:** no human cohort/item calibration, longitudinal reliability or true skill-transfer evidence. Blocks normalized lab scores and general improvement guarantees.
6. **Market collateral/void/correlation:** per-market LMSR math is sound under assumptions; portfolio, rounding and exceptional settlement semantics need complete service-level proof/tests.
7. **Integrated economy and pacing:** funded toy transfer fixes conservation only; dynamic firms/prizes/edge/recovery may still dominate or stall. Needs integrated scenario grid.
8. **Long-run world/rating dynamics:** stationary tests omit retirements/entrants/learning/promotion selection; anchors and style diversity untested at scale.
9. **Lifetime history durability:** Safari eviction and user backup behavior remain irreducible risks without cloud. External backup UX and restore must work; no promise of impossible persistence.
10. **Breadth and cognitive load:** the design is coherent as a knowledge loop but still large; no playtest proves enjoyable hundred-hour engagement. Stage execution, progressive disclosure and user study before expanding presentation complexity.

## Research incompleteness vs implementation freedom

Blocked scientific articles were not read; actual mobile/browser/Windows benchmarks were not run. ExperimentH measures affordability, not real rank pacing; J is hosted Python, not mobile feasibility. No full implemented cancellation/history-versioning service exists. These are explicit unfinished work, not optional polish. The package supplies implementation choices and gates rather than pretending all evidence exists.

Do not remove a requested system solely to close the plan. Simplify its first implementation (restricted condition tree, template coach, tiered simulator) while preserving the full acceptance intent. A future editor should update decisions with reason, evidence and migration implications. Historical experimental data and negative findings remain retained.

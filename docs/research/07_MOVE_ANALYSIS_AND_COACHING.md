# Move analysis, accuracy and trustworthy local coaching

This document operationalizes `requirements/INTELLIGENCE_EXPANSION.md`; the preserved expansion remains binding. Analyze every game asynchronously and retain full move-aligned evidence. Analysis is assistance, and using candidate moves during play makes that game assisted/unrated.

## Score semantics

Store typed `cp`, `mate` (signed plies), or exact terminal WDL. Every score includes perspective, engine/settings, requested/achieved depth and bound type. At root position P, evaluate all legal candidates using identical engine/search policy and record best continuation Q_best and played Q_played. Also retain before/after board evaluations. Prefer root-constrained searches for candidates so “loss” is not an artifact of unequal depth; a child re-search must use a documented comparable policy. Differences within instability bands are not meaningful ordering evidence. Negative estimated losses are clamped to zero for aggregation but flagged as search inconsistency, not hidden.

## Accuracy v0-experimental [D/H]

A transparent initial expected-score map is u(e)=1/(1+exp(−e/s)), with a provisional s=200cp. This is a **placeholder model parameter for research**, not a calibrated variant truth. Production calibration must fit a monotone map on variant-specific self-play outcomes under defined strength/time controls, held out by opening/game family; consider WDL-aware multinomial calibration and isotonic alternatives. Brier/log loss and reliability curves choose calibration, not aesthetic score distribution.

For eligible nonforced decisions define L_i=max(0,u(best_i)−u(played_i)) in[0,1]. Accuracy=100*exp(−k*mean(L_i)), provisional k=4. Equal decision weights avoid post-hoc flattering weights; future weighting requires a new method version. This bounded score has minimum100e^−4 for all maximal-loss decisions, which is intentional in v0; it need not attain zero merely because the display range is0–100. Empty decisions return null. Ordinary forced single-legal moves are reported but excluded from overall accuracy and special-credit frequencies; state their counts. Missing analysis yields partial coverage, not perfect play. Never average raw cp losses into “accuracy.”

Mate scores do not enter the cp transform: exact terminal win/draw/loss maps to1/.5/0; heuristic mates use a mate-evidence category with search stability and disclose nonproof. Mate distance breaks ties separately: slower verified mate preserving a win is not a blunder solely due to distance. For stable heuristic mate scores use u=1 for a mate favorable to the mover and u=0 for unfavorable mate, visibly marking the entire affected accuracy/classification provisional. A stable mate and a cp score compare through these same u values; cp never receives an artificial mate centipawn value. Stable means the mate sign and winning side agree in two successive larger-budget searches, not a proof. If either score is missing/unstable, that move contributes no accuracy and the report is partial. Pure mate-preserving speed changes have L=0 and a separate mate-distance annotation. If mate evidence is unstable, no confident accuracy/classification. Drawn solved states remain. Store native cp loss only for cp/ cp comparisons, with mate changes reported separately.

Phases [D]: determine the phase of move n from its pre-move state P[n−1]. Opening ends before move13 or after the first position with no queen for either side, whichever earlier. A queen capture ending one side's queen presence counts; this is not an undefined “queen exchange.” Endgame begins at the first subsequent position with both queens absent and at most four nonpawn nonking pieces. Phase transitions are monotone (opening→middlegame→endgame, possibly skipping middlegame) and never reverse after promotion. Once endgame begins, later queen promotions do not reclassify history; an endgame material subtype may change. Before any transition, apply all qualifying transitions to the pre-move state, then classify the move. Rules are deterministic but need variant validation. Absent phases show unavailable. Critical positions are defined before examining the played move: at least two legal moves and a top-vs-next plausible-candidate expected-score gap >=.10, or a verified tactical threat/mate transition. Store selection provenance and report opportunity performance.

## Classification v0-experimental [D/H]

First validate legality/completion/stability. If uncertain, display a provisional label plus why or leave pending. Fixed loss bins use L: [0,.002] Best/equivalent, (.002,.02] Excellent, (.02,.05] Good, (.05,.12] Inaccuracy, (.12,.25] Mistake, (.25,1] Blunder. Compare inclusive upper boundaries with numeric tolerance1e-9 and clamp L to[0,1]. Great/Brilliant eligibility requires L<=.002. These numeric boundaries do not shift based on uncertainty. Separately store a loss interval from repeated-budget score ranges. If that interval crosses a label boundary, show “uncertain: {possible labels}” with a provisional point label, and withhold Great/Brilliant. Search variation ranges are sensitivity intervals, not statistical95% confidence unless a sampling model supports that interpretation. These thresholds are explicit experimental choices requiring calibration and human review before “validated” language.

Near-best moves are eligible for Great/Brilliant in precedence Brilliant→Great→Best only after verification. Great requires multiple legal choices, a best-vs-next viable gap >=.10 and stable best status at two increasing search budgets; a legally forced move gets Best with forced annotation and no special credit. Brilliant additionally requires a nontrivial verified tactic, such as a voluntary material investment whose strongest tested defenses lead to regained material, forced mate or sustained advantage, compared with alternatives. Search at a stronger verification budget and record branches; if no practical bounded verification succeeds, withhold Brilliant. A standard recapture or unexplained sacrifice is insufficient. Numerical gap/sacrifice value criteria are methodology parameters, not scientific definitions.

Store tactical evidence graphs (move, captures, threats, attacked squares, defended replies, outcome) and proof status. Search can support “engine finds…”; only exhaustive/legal proof permits “forced under all legal defenses.” A single PV is never a proof of every defense. Do not claim Great/Brilliant results from the material-only research engine.

## Full report and coach

Report summary, both accuracies, phase accuracy, average cp loss for eligible cp moves, mistakes/blunders, best frequency, critical positions, timeline and evaluation graph. Highlight worst mistake/largest missed opportunity/best verified tactical sequence with exact game/ply links and method. Opening/endgame assessments may be absent. Clicking ply n selects the exact post-move position and shows pre/post evaluations explicitly; principal variations branch from their stated origin. Replaying the mistake starts at ply n−1 with clocks/rules context copied into an unrated training branch.

Local motif detectors: forks (attacks on multiple valuable targets plus legal safety), pins, skewers, discovered attacks, mating nets, hanging pieces and promotion races. Verify proposed explanations by legal branch searches. Template example: “After {move}, {reply} attacks {targets}. The engine prefers {alternative}; continuation {PV}. Search depth {d}; result is heuristic.” Do not infer intention, emotion or strategic understanding from moves.

Recurring pattern criteria [D]: at least five eligible opportunities over at least three games, posterior/cluster interval shown; label tentative until comparison against baseline is credible. Recommend a verified exercise family, record attempts, then compare new unseen opportunities with the pretraining period. Training effectiveness is observational unless randomized; never promise causality. General recommendations are labeled general when history is sparse. Optional local language models may paraphrase verified evidence only after device tests; no mandatory LLM dependency.

## Persistence and reanalysis

Report ID includes game checksum, engine hash, variant/rules, depth/node/time settings, cache-context policy, grading/accuracy/calibration and phase versions. Store immutable old reports; a new engine produces an optional new version. Cache full rules state/history, not just board layout. Cancellation and stale result IDs are tested; historical views never silently migrate accuracy. Compare compatible cohorts or explicitly reanalyze selected games.

Production accuracy calibration and a reviewed exceptional-move corpus remain unfinished research gates. Ship experimental labels only with truthful disclosure; M2 cannot claim validated analytics until the relevant validation passes.

## Unified resources and agency

Chess rating measures play only. Federation prestige records competition and institutional achievements. Forecast skill records probabilistic accuracy separately from trading P&L. Fictional crowns fund optional entries, facilities and scouting conveniences. None buys rating, guaranteed wins, premium analysis depth or training answers. Core analysis and practice remain free.

Specialists can ignore wealth. Puzzle players earn academy certificates through held-out assessments without acquiring tournament titles. Traders can research and forecast NPC-only events without playing chess. Club owners choose hosting, specialization and budgets without mandatory board play. Hybrid players gain breadth of choices, not an automatic efficiency multiplier that invalidates specialization.

## First ten minutes [D: experience target, not measured timing]

First launch: local-only disclosure, backup reminder, board orientation, motion/contrast settings, choose Play/Learn/Watch. No account. A three-position optional diagnostic is provisional and cannot produce an authoritative skill profile. A compact board overlay explains one-step pawns and the absence of bishops/castling. Start an adjustable untimed friendly against a novice agent; assistance is visible and the game is unrated.

On victory, show the actual turning point, not generic praise. On defeat, show one recoverable mistake and offer retry from the position. The report records both outcomes permanently. First rated game is offered after rules confidence; show the expected rating uncertainty before play. Never force a win or rig hidden difficulty mid-game.

First report: board, one turning point, actual pending/completed analysis status, rating explanation when eligible. First tactical session uses a verified motif from the game or explicitly labeled curated content if no suitable mistake exists. The player can leave immediately; no reward lost for stopping.

## First hour

Choose a short local event or continue training. First tournament is a four-player round robin with visible schedule and free entry; funded prizes have modest fixed budgets. First rivalry emerges from repeated close competitive encounters, not fabricated dialogue about nonexistent games. First market trade is optional: a capped-size tutorial on a different NPC match explains average execution cost, fees, payout and worst-case loss before confirmation. A paper forecast alternative requires no bankroll. No trade is required to unlock competitive progress.

## First hundred hours

Seasons expose stronger qualification fields, positional opponents, thematic preparation and novel endgames. Championships require event qualification, not hours played. The first meaningful institution investment selects academy, scouting or hosting opportunities with upkeep and a visible opportunity cost. Rival adaptations depend on observed openings/style, never unseen player moves.

A serious setback can mean relegation, an event loss or a failed portfolio. The response is a concrete recovery path: free rated events/training, an institutional budget restructuring, and restricted practice credit rather than profitable bankruptcy grants. Historical titles remain recorded even if rank falls. Long-term endgame is sustained competitive excellence, forecast skill, a Hall of Fame institution and a personal library of demonstrable improvement. No infinite numeric prestige reset is required.

## Behavioral rationale and validation

Autonomy: optional paths, clear exits, reversible preferences. Competence: honest feedback, attainable challenges, meaningful uncertainty. Relatedness: persistent rival histories and club identity [R: sdt]. These are applications, not proof of retention. Flow, deliberate practice, challenge bands and goal-setting are hypotheses here pending usability/play studies. Offer suggested opponents with estimated expected score near 0.4–0.6 [D], allow override, never manipulate outcomes.

No streak loss, timed reward collection, loot boxes, deceptive odds, forced daily play, or escalating loss-chasing prompts. Defeat feedback is calm. Markets have exposure limits and an optional hide toggle. Gather opt-in local playtest notes about comprehension, agency, fatigue, perceived fairness and whether players choose another game; do not optimize solely for session duration. Human enjoyment remains unverified.

# Independent behavioral-science and learning-measurement review

Scope: documents02,03,06,07,13, with emphasis on evidence honesty and measurement interpretation. This is an independent reasoning review within the same agent system, not external expert certification or a human-subject validation study.

## Assessment

The package properly distinguishes its own design judgments from primary research and synthetic observations. It explicitly records inaccessible articles, avoids IQ-transfer claims, separates trading performance from chess ability, and refuses to fabricate skill scores. Those safeguards should remain binding. It is a useful **conditional implementation blueprint**, not completed multidisciplinary validation: the psychology evidence base and real psychometric calibration remain unfinished.

## Required corrections

1. **High — Success on an opportunity does not identify the cognitive process.** A successful defense can follow lucky selection, remembered repertoire or deliberate calculation; failure can reflect execution or clock limits rather than failure to notice a threat. Display “performance on verified threat-response opportunities,” not an unqualified measured mental faculty. Ten dashboard dimensions are content-domain summaries until discriminant/construct validity is established. Do not imply that tactical vision, calculation and threat detection have been empirically separated.

2. **High — ExperimentI demonstrates confounding by construction, not a validated correction.** The held-out success rate is explicitly generated at.60 for easier-opponent/memorization scenarios. It therefore cannot show that a real matching, leakage detector or statistical adjustment recovered ability correctly. Keep the current limitation and add a requirement to validate the actual item-family exclusion/difficulty-adjustment code on a synthetic data generator with latent ability, item difficulty, exposure and correlated families. Report parameter recovery, interval coverage, false-positive rate and power. Do not citeI as proof of the production assessment method.

3. **High — Repeated looks and ten simultaneous dimensions need an operational decision policy.** General warnings are insufficient for an implementation prompt. Descriptive intervals may update continuously, but a headline “improved” assertion should use prespecified nonoverlapping windows and either a defined familywise/FDR correction or a reviewed sequential inference method. Suggested first policy: one comparison at each completed30-game block, Holm correction across the ten prespecified primary dimensions at familywise alpha=.05, with a minimum practically meaningful change and interval estimate. Insufficient opportunities yields no assertion. These numbers are proposed design choices requiring simulation, not established best values.

4. **High — The grading uncertainty algorithm is still underspecified.** Document07 says eps expands Best and shifts bins “consistently” without a formula. This can yield incompatible labels between implementations and hide engine instability. A concrete experimental option: store a loss interval from matched repeated-search estimates; assign a confident loss label only when the whole interval lies within one fixed bin. Otherwise show the adjacent candidate labels and “uncertain.” Best is a claim of estimated equivalence, not proof of optimality. Do not portray depth stability as a calibrated confidence probability.

5. **Medium — A normal-CDF score is not automatically a reference percentile.**100Phi(theta) is a model-normalized display transform. An empirical percentile requires a representative frozen reference distribution and linkage across item versions; a normal latent prior does not create that distribution. Keep descriptive rates until the cohort/item calibration exists. If model-normalized scores are later used, label them separately from empirical cohort percentiles.

6. **Medium — Opportunity denominators are endogenous.** Players choose openings, opponents and whether to simplify into endgames, so an improving conditional success rate need not show broad skill growth. Store selection context, report opportunity composition changes and use matched held-out tasks for higher-confidence ability claims. Cluster by game/family, not just move; stratification by opponent rating/time control alone does not eliminate selection bias.

7. **Medium — Accuracy and postgame feedback can cause misleading learning incentives.** Equal averaging of bounded outcome loss has saturation and move-composition effects. A player may score highly in an already lost game, or improve apparent accuracy through familiar/easy positions. Keep accuracy as conditional decision quality; never use it alone to rank development, claim training success or grant skill titles. Report critical-position and conversion measures alongside it. Avoid maximizing the decorative Brilliant count as an academy goal.

## Statistical-reporting refinements

The across-run CIs in13 use1.96*SE; for twelve independent runs, a t interval is preferable and the current interval should remain labeled a normal approximation. At n=10 per assessment window, pooled z testing has weak approximation properties;I already discloses this. Production testing should use appropriate exact/model-based alternatives and verify coverage rather than copying the exploratory z rule.

Equal seeds acrossI scenarios are paired/common-random-number trials, which is useful for comparing scenarios but means identical rows are expected by construction. Do not treat them as independent replications of a learned correction. All quantitative conclusions should retain their sampling unit and assumptions.

## Human validation gate

Before claiming the core loop is enjoyable or the laboratory measures distinct skills, conduct a documented small formative playtest (consent, no inference beyond its sample), revise onboarding/feedback, then a prospective longitudinal pilot with fixed outcomes and held-out item families. Record baseline difficulty, familiarity, time control, assistance and missingness. No invented sample size or promise of statistical power belongs in the release claim. The absence of a human cohort is an unresolved research requirement, not evidence against the product.

## Disposition recommendation

Proceed with foundational implementation and transparent descriptive analytics. Keep calibrated score normalization, formal improvement assertions and construct-level marketing gated. Preserve all requested product systems; stage them without claiming scientific validity they have not yet earned.

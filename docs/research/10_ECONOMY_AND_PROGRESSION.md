# Economy, ownership and recovery

## Design principles

Fictional crowns buy choices, convenience and institutional opportunities; they never buy stronger legal moves, rating, analysis truth or championship qualification. Chess-only and academy-only careers stay viable. Portfolio P&L, chess earnings, club prestige and forecasts are separately legible; a hybrid does not automatically double all rewards.

A unified double-entry ledger represents grants, tournament entry/prizes, sponsorship, trade/settlement, fees, institutions, operating costs, mint/burn and reserves. Distinguish currency creation/destruction from transfers. NPC wallets and maker subsidy are part of the same economy, not invisible unlimited cash.

## Flows and opportunities [D]

Free public matches/training/basic events always available. Paid special events consume time/entry cost but prizes are escrowed before registration; cancel/withdrawal rules published. Sponsors pay for earned milestones from season budgets, not infinite repeatable achievements. Prize pools are transfers, not currency minted on every win. New-season treasury issuance is explicit, bounded and logged; use an economic monitor before expanding it.

Institutions have capped capacity, upkeep and strategic opportunity costs:

- Chess club: hosting rights, rival/community identity and event access, not guaranteed passive yield.
- Training facility: organizes practice plans and expands exercise curation; core analysis remains free.
- Analyst desk: saved opponent dossiers and research queue convenience; no privileged future results.
- Scouting: alternative opponent/entrant reports with disclosed uncertainty, not hidden perfect strength.
- Research department: opening/position exploration workflows under the same accessible engine limits.
- Tournament hosting: commit an escrow budget, choose format and schedule; prestige depends on completed fair events and attendance, costs and revenue disclosed.

Ownership returns derive from actual attendance/sponsorship/hosting and operating costs, with capacity limits and competition. No exponential idle-income multiplier. Investments lock capital and create opportunity; they cannot dominate playing, trading and research simultaneously. Player-controlled institutions cannot trade on their own influenced events.

## Recovery

Bankruptcy means cash at or near zero; no margin debt or permanent exclusion. Ordinary chess/training and paper forecasts stay available. Recovery offers a free tournament path and restricted nontransferable academy/entry vouchers issued once per scheduled season under an explicit budget. Vouchers cannot buy tradeable shares, be sold to NPCs, generate market collateral or be repeatedly redeemed by deliberate losses. Restructure/close optional facilities to stop upkeep; protect core history and earned titles. A fresh independent career is optional and does not erase the old record.

## Experiments and corrections

G/H tests five toy policies over100 careers each,120 seasons. Fixed positive grants/prizes without effective sinks accumulate wealth; this is a failure signal, not balanced tuning. It models Gaussian trading transfers, not actual LMSR agents, and lets the baseline mint rescue money without a cap. No claim of non-dominance follows.

Follow-up `data/economy_retest.json` makes transfers fully funded, keeps reserve/player cash nonnegative and checks total-money conservation across60 seeded runs and three upkeep rates. This fixes **accounting creation**, not progression fairness. It still lacks actual tournament outcomes, club revenue, forecast edge and correlated markets. Before M4, implement the integrated model and report wealth quantiles/Gini, currency issuance/sinks, bankruptcies, recovery time, affordability, reserve insolvency and strategy utility across parameter grids.

Pacing target [D/H]: first optional funded event within first hour; first meaningful club commitment after sustained career evidence rather than grind; championship access through skill. No hours-to-title promise can be inferred from cash simulation. H's seasons-to1000 measures affordability only. Rank promotion pacing must use actual improving, stationary, declining and returning player trajectories with uncertain ratings and tournament qualification.

## Anti-dominance checks

Compare chess-only, puzzle-only, conservative analyst, aggressive trader, hybrid, institutional specialist, struggling and expert cohorts. Include market edge decay, fees, drawdowns, bounded prizes, upkeep, time/attention budgets and limited sponsor contracts. If one strategy wins every wealth/prestige/skill objective, introduce a real opportunity cost rather than a hidden penalty. Preserve multiple definitions of success; equal wealth is not required for equal viability.

Long-run inflation control is a policy choice: target affordability relative to career stage and treasury budget, avoid covert item-price scaling with personal wealth. Publish economic changes and preserve historical ledger versions. No monetization or manipulative retention system belongs here.

## Fully costed first institution reference scenario [D/H]

A Starter Club project requires500 crowns:300 locked one-season operating bond plus200 consumed setup/art/venue cost. Capacity16 members; one four-player weekend event per simulated season. Before confirming, show all cash flows: base upkeep40/season, hosting cost20/event, maximum escrowed prize40, attendance revenue5 per attending member (0–16), sponsor payment at most30 only for a completed nonconflicted event under a funded sponsor contract. No event: bond can fund upkeep, no attendance revenue. Full attendance/completed event: revenue80+30, cash costs40+20+40=100, operating margin10 before setup amortization; zero attendance/no sponsor with a committed event loses100. Prize and attendance are transfers from actual funded accounts, not minted yield. Owner entry fees and own funded prizes cannot farm net money.

Bond can be released only after settling all project obligations; a shortfall suspends hosting and offers restructuring, not invisible debt. One club occupies the player's sole initial management slot and foregoes using500 cash as trading/event capital. This scenario is a reference implementation parameter set, **not balanced tuning**; integrated G/H tests must examine demand, subsidy and ownership conflicts. Academy-only endgame offers verified unseen-item mastery certificates, coaching/curation projects and evidence portfolios with institutional prestige separate from chess titles; certification cannot be bought with club wealth.

# Analysis calibration (M2, Phase B)

Generated by `npx tsx tools/calibration/report.ts` from `tools/calibration/fit-results.json`.

## Question

Move grades and accuracy use an expected-outcome map u(cp) from engine evaluation to expected score.
v0 used the research placeholder u = 1/(1+e^(−cp/200)). Is a fitted map better on held-out 6×6 data?

## Corpus (`tools/calibration/build_corpus.ts`, id crown-calib-corpus-1)

- 767 benchmark games between near-equal Crown configurations (|measured Elo difference| < 75),
  so outcomes reflect positions rather than strength mismatches.
- Every 3rd ply from ply 6: 19187 positions scored by the Crown engine (depth 6, side-to-move view),
  labelled with the final result for the side to move (1 / ½ / 0). 18411 centipawn-scored, 776 mate-scored.
- Independence groups: 10 opening families (White's first move: a2a3, b1a3, b1c3, b2b3, c2c3, d2d3, e1d3, e1f3, e2e3, f2f3).
  **Leave-one-family-out cross-validation**: every position is predicted by a model fitted without its family.

### Mate scores

Positions with an engine mate score for the side to move scored 1.000 on average (n = 450);
mate against the side to move scored 0.000 (n = 326). This supports keeping mates outside the cp
map with u = 1 / 0 as specified, but these are engine-vs-engine conversions, not proofs.

## Decision

**ISO isotonic (PAV)** has the lowest held-out Brier score and its game-bootstrap interval for the Brier difference against P0 lies entirely below zero (-0.00476 to -0.00201).

The analyser therefore publishes **crown-accuracy-v1** with expected-score map **crown-outcome-map-v1**: the isotonic curve refitted on all 18411 cp positions, reduced to monotone knots and linearly interpolated (`src/analysis/outcome-map-v1.json`, `tools/calibration/publish_v1.ts`). The fitted logistic (s ≈ 176 cp) was **not** adopted because its improvement interval includes zero: a single scale cannot express what the data show — on 6×6, small advantages convert more often than s = 200 predicts while large ones saturate, and the side to move scores ≈ 0.57 at an even evaluation. New reports use grading id `crown-grade-v1-experimental`; old v0 reports keep their own map for display and are never merged with v1 in the Lab.

What remains experimental even after this fit:
- The data are engine-vs-engine games at ~40k nodes per move; human games may convert advantages differently.
- Grade bin boundaries (0.002 / 0.02 / 0.05 / 0.12 / 0.25) and accuracy k = 4 are **not** re-fitted — there is no
  human-labelled grade data to fit them against. They stay labelled experimental.
- Great/Brilliant remain heuristic, gated by stability, uniqueness and deeper verification.

## Grade regression corpus (Gate B)

`tests/fixtures/grades.json` (26 fixtures, settings quick-d5): real positions with real candidate moves analysed by the production analyser.
Labels covered: great, blunder, best, mistake, inaccuracy, brilliant, good, excellent. Missing (no clean example found): brilliant:1.

checks every boundary of every bin, Great/Brilliant gating, uncertainty labelling and mate handling on synthetic inputs.
